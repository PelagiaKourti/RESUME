## Selected work

### [Geometry of Quantum Computing](https://github.com/PelagiaKourti/MSc-Thesis-Quantum-Computing) — MSc Thesis
The quantum geometric tensor and its decomposition into the Fubini–Study metric and Berry curvature,
using tools from symplectic geometry and Kähler manifolds; the relationship between the quantum geometric
tensor and the (quantum) Fisher information; the Hopf algebra structure arising from the interacting
Frobenius algebras of the ZX-calculus, and circuit optimization with the ZX-rules.

### [Optimum Chocolates' Distribution](https://github.com/PelagiaKourti/chocolates-distribution)
An NP-hard combinatorial problem: distributing bars of integer lengths among children with integer
demands using the fewest cuts. A polynomial-time heuristic that avoids exponential search.

### [Introduction to the Geometry of Special Relativity](https://github.com/PelagiaKourti/BSc-Thesis-Special-Relativity) — BSc Thesis
The geometric structure of Lorentz–Minkowski space: relativistic transformations, isometries and causal structure.

### [Proximity Game Player](https://github.com/PelagiaKourti/Proximity-Game)
A Python player for the Proximity board game that evaluates the board and chooses the optimal move each turn.
